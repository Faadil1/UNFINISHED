# PDPB BUILDER GATE

Authorization condition: the active Prototype/Kill Test must PASS.

## Required PASS evidence
1. Recognition: inherited states are legible without explanatory text doing the work.
2. Counterfactual Authorship: the same completion yields materially different play depending on the prior person's state.
3. Human Necessity: HUMAN attribution and causality matter more than WORLD/system attribution.
4. Playfulness: testers physically use the resulting topology rather than treating the experience as a configurator.
