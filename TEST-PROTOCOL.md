# Cold Test Protocol

## Setup
Use a phone if possible. Do not explain the idea. Say only:

**“Essaie ceci et dis-moi ce que tu comprends.”**

Do not say social, co-author, previous player, unfinished, relay, or persistence before the run.

## Observe silently
1. Does the tester notice inherited geometry before attribution text?
2. Does ARC vs STEP affect how they choose CONNECT vs RISE?
3. After the morph, do they immediately move into/use the topology?
4. Can they explain why the previous contributor mattered?
5. Do they understand their final stem is for someone else?

## Post-test questions
- Qu'est-ce que la personne précédente a fait ?
- Qu'est-ce que toi tu as fait ?
- Si la première forme avait été différente, est-ce que ton expérience aurait changé ?
- Qu'est-ce qui t'a donné envie — ou pas — d'utiliser ce qui est apparu ?

## Hard kill conditions
- 2+ of first 5 testers say the prior human is irrelevant.
- 2+ of first 5 describe it only as a shape/configuration menu.
- fewer than 4/5 use the topology after reveal.
- HUMAN and WORLD are described as essentially equivalent.
- prior stem does not change practical traversal consequence.

PASS → PDPB Builder.
PIVOT → change only the failed primitive and retest.
KILL → do not automatically fall back to NEED//RELAY or RELAY ECHO.
